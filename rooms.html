Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before arriving in Juba.

Is electricity and air conditioning available 24 hours a day?

Yes. Juba's municipal grid is supplemented by our two heavy-duty industrial diesel generators operating in tandem, guaranteeing 100% uninterrupted electricity, hot water, and AC.

How do I schedule my airport pickup?

Simply provide your flight number (e.g. Kenya Airways, Ethiopian Airlines, or Fly540) and arrival time when submitting your booking request or via our WhatsApp front desk.

Are meals and beverages available on-site?

Our on-site restaurant serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner daily, featuring fresh East African specialties, grilled meats, and international dishes, plus room service until 22:00.

Can you host private meetings or security delegations?

Yes, our Executive Suites and conference boardrooms offer confidential, secure spaces with high-speed internet and catering for delegations and business teams.
